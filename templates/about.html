I About the Project

THE SOLVER is a free, mysterious math engine that solves any equation you throw at it — step by step, without noise.

No ads. No sign-up. No verbose explanations. Just the math.

◆

How It Works

When you submit a problem, THE SOLVER tries three layers in order:

  1. Calculator — simple arithmetic is solved instantly in Python, without touching any AI.
  2. Cache — if the exact question was asked in the last 24 hours, the answer returns immediately.
  3. AI Engine — complex problems go to one of four engines, with automatic fallback if one fails.

The Four Engines

  • Lightning — fast, general-purpose. Powered by Groq.
  • Reasoning — deep logic for hard problems. Powered by Groq.
  • Gemini — Google's model via OpenRouter.
  • DeepSeek — math specialist via OpenRouter.

Features

  • Step-by-step solutions in LaTeX
  • Solve mode and continuous Chat mode
  • Automatic graph plotting for functions
  • Built-in math keyboard (no need for special input)
  • Answer history (stored locally in your browser)
  • Share answers with a link

Privacy

We don't store your questions on the server. We don't require an account. The only thing kept in memory is an anonymous, temporary cache of past answers to speed things up — cleared automatically every 24 hours.
